A few useful things.

What is included in this preview?

Loki has a searchable shelf, profiles, groups and experimental divider hiding. Always-hidden tiers, replica icon bars, system-bar styling and per-display automation are not included. The repository maintains a feature-by-feature ledger.

Why does it ask for Accessibility?

To discover menu extras and click the one you choose. App shortcuts work without it. Accessibility is a broad macOS permission; the source and privacy document explain Loki’s intended use. Screen Recording is not requested.

How do I reveal everything?

Hover near the menu bar, open Loki, or right-click its icon and select “Reveal All Menu Bar Items.” Quitting Loki also removes its divider. The CLI offers a reveal-all command.

Is it original and open source?

Yes. Loki’s implementation and vector artwork are available under MIT. Loki is independently developed. The folded-rune concept used AI-assisted design exploration. No trademark clearance is claimed.
